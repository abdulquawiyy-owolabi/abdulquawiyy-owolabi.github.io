04 — Case study

Inside the GISorb internship

RoleResearch Intern, Geospatial Data Science
DurationAug 2024 – Mar 2025
LocationRemote
FocusOil spill detection, Niger Delta

My internship at GISorb ran remotely from August 2024 to March 2025. I worked across the firm's environmental-impact projects, which spanned oil spill monitoring in the Niger Delta and ecological assessment work like mangrove ecosystem studies.

Day to day, my responsibilities included collecting Synthetic Aperture Radar data (Sentinel-1 GRD), preparing it in SNAP through calibration and geometric correction for oil spill detection, annotating satellite imagery to build machine-learning training datasets, and running literature reviews on emerging geospatial methods.

Detecting oil spills from orbit

Spill detection combines optical and radar satellite imagery, onsite vessel monitoring, and machine learning. Each Sentinel acquisition was radiometrically calibrated, speckle-filtered, and geometrically corrected in SNAP, then tiled across roughly 100 scenes using a Python pipeline in Jupyter Notebook.

The tiled imagery was annotated in ArcGIS Pro's Label Objects for Deep Learning tool to extract ships, land, sea look-alikes, and spill features across the Niger Delta study area, producing training datasets for Random Forest and SVM classifiers. Model outputs were validated against ground-truth spill locations.

Tracking land-use change over 25 years

To connect spills to downstream agricultural impact, I applied the LandTrendr algorithm in Google Earth Engine to a 1999–2024 Landsat time series, detecting temporal shifts in vegetation and cross-referencing change areas against documented spill locations to visualise the spatial-temporal pattern of land degradation.

Alongside the technical work, I ran literature reviews on adjacent environmental topics — mangrove ecosystem dynamics, the climate impact of inland lakes — to support the wider team's project pipeline. The internship sharpened my grounding in SAR processing, remote-sensing time series analysis, and applying geospatial ML to real environmental problems.
