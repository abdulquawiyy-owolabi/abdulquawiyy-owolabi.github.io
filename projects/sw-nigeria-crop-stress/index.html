How to read this map

Click any cell for its location, local skill and full time series. Arrow keys step through time; space plays or pauses.

Each square is a 0.05° rainfall cell (about 5 km). Values are the share of sample points in the cell that are stressed (observed) or predicted stressed.

Observed is a satellite proxy (an unusually low NDVI for the time of year), not ground truth. Predicted is the out-of-fold probability from model climate+ndvi+s1|xgb at a 0-day lead: each prediction comes from a model that never saw that year group or that spatial block. It is a stress risk, not a yield or damage forecast.

Skill is real for same-window detection; useful lead time beyond about 16 days is not demonstrated. Use this to prioritise where to look, not as an alarm.
